Cable Rear-Delt Rope Pull-Apart

Primary Muscles: Rear Deltoids
Secondary Muscles: None (pure isolation)

Overview

The Cable Rear-Delt Rope Pull-Apart is a horizontal abduction movement performed with a rope attachment. It isolates the rear delts without external rotation, without backward elbow travel, and without upper-back involvement.

Setup

  • Set the pulley at shoulder height.
  • Attach a rope.
  • Step back 3–4 feet until the cable pulls your hands slightly inward.
  • Stand tall with a slight hip hinge and soft knees.
  • Arms straight and parallel to the floor.

Execution

  • Begin with hands slightly inside shoulder width.
  • Pull the rope apart, not back.
  • Keep elbows in line with your shoulders.
  • Do not let elbows travel behind your torso.
  • Stop when hands reach outside shoulder width.
  • Control the negative for 2 seconds.

Key Cues

  • “Hands OUT, not BACK.”
  • “Arms stay parallel to the floor.”
  • “No external rotation.”
  • “Rear delts only — no traps.”

Common Mistakes

  • Pulling backward (turns into a reverse fly or face pull).
  • Letting the arms drift upward.
  • Using too much weight.
  • Shrugging the traps.
